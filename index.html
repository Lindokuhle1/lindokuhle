Who I Am

I'm a Telemetry Support Analyst and Junior Backend Developer based in Durban, South Africa. My work sits at the intersection of backend engineering and IoT — building the systems that collect, process, and surface data from real-world deployments.

Day-to-day I monitor live Li-ion battery fleets via ThingsBoard and Node-RED, provision telemetry systems for client sites, validate BMS firmware, and automate operational reporting through AWS-based workflows. On the development side, I build ASP.NET Core APIs structured around Clean Architecture principles.

I hold a Diploma in ICT (Application Development) from DUT and am currently completing my Advanced Diploma. My longer-term goal is to contribute to scalable, cloud-integrated backend platforms at an international level.
